Bettas—most commonly referring to Betta splendens, or Siamese fighting fish—are popular freshwater aquarium fish native to Southeast Asia (including Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Malaysia, and Indonesia).

In the wild, Bettas inhabit shallow, slow-moving, or stagnant waters such as rice paddies, marshes, and overgrown ponds that are often low in dissolved oxygen...!
